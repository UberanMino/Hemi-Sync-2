Mino Automation

Mino Automation is a private, non-commercial tool used by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels: uploading his own videos, setting thumbnails and playlists, editing video descriptions and reading his own channel analytics through the YouTube API Services.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in with his own Google accounts.
