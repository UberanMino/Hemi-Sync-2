Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Mino Automation ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Data of the owner's own YouTube channels: video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status), playlists, thumbnails and channel analytics.
  • OAuth tokens that allow the app to act on these channels.

How the data is used

Only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to evaluate the performance of his own channels. The app does not collect data about other YouTube users and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored as secrets in the owner's private automation environment and are never published. Analytics and metadata are kept only as long as needed for the owner's channel work.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Stored tokens then stop working.

Datenschutzerklärung (Kurzfassung)

Mino Automation ist ein privates Werkzeug, mit dem der Inhaber ausschließlich seine eigenen YouTube-Kanäle verwaltet (Videos hochladen, Thumbnails, Playlists, Beschreibungen, Kanalstatistiken). Es werden keine Daten anderer Personen erhoben und keine Daten an Dritte weitergegeben. Zugriffstoken liegen nur als Geheimnisse in der privaten Automatisierungsumgebung. Der Zugriff lässt sich jederzeit in den Google-Sicherheitseinstellungen widerrufen.
